Common Reasons a Bonus Is Not Credited

FAQ

Is the bonus free money?

Not necessarily. Promotional credit often has eligibility, gameplay and withdrawal conditions.

Can I withdraw a bonus immediately?

Only if the current terms explicitly allow it. Check wagering and maximum cash-out rules first.

Does every new account receive the same amount?

Offers can vary by time, account and region. Use the dated table above rather than an old advertised figure.

Does a bonus remove the risk of losing money?

No. Bonus play can still result in loss and may encourage additional spending. Set a limit before claiming any offer.
