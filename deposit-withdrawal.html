Before You Deposit

  • Confirm that the app was installed from the verified source.
  • Open the cashier or wallet inside the app, not from a link sent in chat.
  • Check the payee name and amount before approving payment.
  • Read bonus conditions if a promotion is selected.
  • Save the order ID, UTR/reference number and confirmation screenshot.
  • Start with an amount you can afford to lose.

Never send a Diwa Top payment to a replacement UPI ID or personal account supplied by an unverified agent. If payment details change unexpectedly during checkout, cancel and confirm the change through official support.

How to Deposit

The exact button labels must match the current app:

  1. Log in to the verified account.
  2. Open Wallet, Cashier or Deposit.
  3. Choose one of the verified payment methods.
  4. Enter an amount within the displayed limit.
  5. Review the recipient and payment request.
  6. Approve the transaction in the payment app.
  7. Return to the game app and wait for the order status to update.

Do not repeat the same payment immediately if the balance is delayed. Check the bank status and payment reference first.

How to Request a Withdrawal

  1. Open the wallet and select Withdraw.
  2. Complete any required identity or bank verification.
  3. Confirm that the name on the payment account matches the registered details.
  4. Enter an amount within the current limit.
  5. Review any wagering, turnover or bonus restriction.
  6. Submit the request and save the withdrawal/order ID.
  7. Monitor the status inside the app rather than through a third-party agent.

Withdrawal Pending

A pending status may result from bank processing, account review, incorrect details, bonus conditions or an operator delay. Collect the account ID, order ID, amount, submission time and redacted screenshot, then contact verified support.

Do not pay an extra “tax,” “unlock fee” or “security deposit” merely because a private message demands it. If an official fee exists, it should be visible in the operator’s published terms and transaction screen.

Deposit Debited but Balance Not Added

  • Check whether the bank marks the transaction successful, pending or reversed.
  • Confirm the UTR/reference number.
  • Compare the payee with the one displayed in the app.
  • Wait for the stated reconciliation period.
  • Submit one clear support ticket with evidence.
  • Do not post full bank details, OTPs or UPI PINs.

Rejected or Reversed Withdrawal

Review the account name, bank/UPI details, KYC status, minimum amount and bonus turnover. Ask support for the exact rejection reason in writing. A generic request to “deposit more first” is not enough explanation.

Payment Safety

Gaming transactions can be lost through gameplay, operator failure, fraud or user error. A successful test withdrawal does not guarantee every future withdrawal. Keep deposits within a fixed entertainment budget and stop using the platform if payment instructions, support identities or terms become inconsistent.

FAQ

What is the minimum withdrawal?

Open the withdraw screen — the minimum shown there is the one that counts. It can change after updates.

How long does a withdrawal take?

Timing is whatever the app and your bank show. We don’t promise “instant” cashouts.

Can support ask for my UPI PIN or OTP?

No. Those credentials should never be shared.

Is a withdrawal guaranteed after I deposit?

No. Eligibility, terms, account checks and operator risk all apply. Users should not deposit money they cannot afford to lose.
