Information to Include in a Ticket

  • registered account ID or masked mobile number;
  • issue category;
  • date and time;
  • app version;
  • transaction or round ID;
  • amount involved;
  • a redacted screenshot;
  • a short description of the expected and actual result.

Do not include passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, full bank details or unmasked identity documents in a public message.

Login Complaint

Explain whether the problem is an OTP delay, forgotten password, changed number, locked account or unfamiliar activity. Follow the login guide before submitting repeated requests.

Deposit or Withdrawal Complaint

Provide the order ID, UTR/reference number, amount, time and current in-app status. Read the payment troubleshooting guide for the evidence checklist.

Game Result Complaint

Record the exact game, round ID, stake, result and time. A screenshot without a round ID may be difficult to investigate. Ask for a written case or ticket number.

How to Recognise Fake Support

  • It contacts you first from an unlisted account.
  • It asks for an OTP, password, UPI PIN or withdrawal PIN.
  • It requests screen-sharing or device-control access.
  • It asks for another deposit to release a withdrawal.
  • It promises guaranteed recovery or winnings.
  • The contact details do not appear inside the verified app.

FAQ

What is the official support number?

Publish a number only when it is verified inside the app or on an operator-controlled source. At present the confirmed channel is In-app support.

Will support ask for an OTP?

It should not. Never disclose an OTP, password or financial PIN.

How long should I wait for a reply?

Use the response time stated by the verified support channel. Keep one ticket thread and avoid sending conflicting details through many unverified accounts.
